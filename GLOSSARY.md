# Glossary

**Vehicle**: A physical bus, coach, ferry or train that reports its position. It keeps its identity across many journeys.

**Journey**: One scheduled trip a vehicle runs, with a line, an origin, a destination and a delay. A vehicle runs one journey at a time and moves on to the next when the trip ends.

**Mode**: The kind of vehicle: bus, coach, ferry or train. Mode picks the vehicle's 3D model.

**Trail**: The fading line behind a moving vehicle, showing where it has been recently. A trail belongs to the vehicle, so it carries on across journey changes.

**Stale vehicle**: A vehicle whose last report is too old to trust, or whose journey is completed or cancelled. Stale vehicles are hidden from the map.

**Mood**: The overall look of the map: lighting, colours and sky. The first mood is night.
